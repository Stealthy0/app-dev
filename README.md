# 🎬 Interstellar (2014)

> *"Mankind was born on Earth. It was never meant to die here."*

Directed by **Christopher Nolan**, *Interstellar* is an epic science-fiction film that explores humanity's fight for survival, the mysteries of spacetime, and the enduring power of love across dimensions.

## ⭐ Key Highlights

* **Scientific Realism:** Developed in consultation with theoretical physicist Kip Thorne, accurately modeling gravitational lensing and the supermassive black hole named `Gargantua`.
* **Iconic Score:** Hans Zimmer's pipe-organ soundtrack perfectly captures both the vastness of space and intimate emotional weight.
* **Core Themes:** Blends hard science with deep emotional stakes centered on family and sacrifice.
